# Experiment 6.b: Cylinder and Sphere Using Interface

## Aim
To calculate the volume of a cylinder and the surface area of a sphere using interfaces.

## Theory
`Cylinder` implements `CylinderShape`, while `Sphere` implements `Shape` to provide the required calculations.

## Programs
`Shape.java`, `CylinderShape.java`, `Cylinder.java`, `Sphere.java`, and `ShapeDemo.java`.

## Result
The cylinder volume and sphere surface area were calculated and displayed successfully.
